## What exists

**B2 is in `LADDER` and in `Level`, not in `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`** (#74). Every `from()` tag already
includes it, deliberately (#76): the day B2 opens, it lists every page from A1 up with no edit.
Nothing is tagged at B2's own floor, and nothing should be until B2 is a decision.

## Before opening it

Opening B2 is adding it to `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`. Three things fail silently if that is all you do:

- **The `perLevel` pages vanish from a B2 listing.** They list exactly the levels they have
  material for — `A2B1` — so a B2 learner loses every `lecture` text and the per-level drills from
  their chapters. Each needs a B2 set, or a decision that B2 goes without it. A B2 face is a **B2
  question set**, never a wider tag (#76).
- **Closing it again is a silent reset** (#74): `readLevel` filters on `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`, so anyone
  who chose B2 reads back as having chosen nothing. **Do not open it to see what it looks like.**
- **The chooser rates nothing** (#77). A B2 with nothing of its own lists exactly what B1 does, and
  nothing says so; while the audience is known that is acceptable, and if the site is ever listed
  the « en cours » note comes back first.
