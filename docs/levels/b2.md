# B2

The context for B2: **declared, not choosable, nothing written at its floor.** The top of the
ladder — C1 and C2 are out of scope, not deferred (#75). The other rungs: [`a1.md`](a1.md),
[`a2.md`](a2.md), [`b1.md`](b1.md).

## What exists

**B2 is in `LADDER` and in `Level`, not in `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`** (#74). A learner on « Tout »
(`view: "all"`) will see it the day it opens (#86). Nothing is tagged B2 until B2 is a decision.

## Before opening it

Opening B2 is adding it to `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`. Four things go wrong if that is all you do, three of
them silently:

- **A B2-only view shows almost nothing** — only pages tagged B2 and pages with no level. The
  reading texts and the drills with `sets` need a **B2 set** (#87), a literary work a **`b2.tsx`
  body** (#92), or a decision that B2 goes
  without; a B2 learner who wants the rest keeps B1 in view.
- **It needs a B2 parcours** (#88), or « La suite » has nothing to say at B2. **This one is
  checked at import**: a choosable level with no parcours fails, and so does a page tagged B2 (or
  a B2 set) that no B2 parcours names. The other three are not.
- **Closing it again is a silent reset** (#74): `readView` filters on `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`, so B2
  drops out of every view that held it. **Do not open it to see what it looks like.**
- **The chooser rates nothing** (#77): a B2 with nothing of its own looks empty, and nothing
  says so. If the site is ever listed, the « en cours » note comes back first.

## When it is written

French and Spanish explanations (#102), measured against DELF B2 (#15) from the *Inventaire* Annexe E. Write its
syllabus map here before its first page, in the shape of [`b1.md`](b1.md)'s. **Not before B1's
gate is mostly crossed**: B1 has no role-play yet, and B2's functions (raconter en détail, défendre
un point de vue, synthétiser des sources) are B1's asked harder. **Do not tighten the
database's level shape** to drop C1 when writing B2 — it holds what a rung looks like, not which
rungs are taught (#75).
