ladder — C1 and C2 are out of scope, not deferred (#75). The other rungs: [`a1.md`](a1.md),

## What exists

**B2 is in `LADDER` and in `Level`, not in `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`** (#74). A learner on « Tout »
(`view: "all"`) will see it the day it opens (#86). Nothing is tagged B2 until B2 is a decision.

## Before opening it

Opening B2 is adding it to `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`. Four things fail silently if that is all you do:

- **A B2-only view shows almost nothing** — only pages tagged B2 and pages with no level. The
  `lecture` texts and the drills with `sets` need a **B2 set** (#87), or a decision that B2 goes
  without; a B2 learner who wants the rest keeps B1 in view.
- **It needs a B2 parcours** (#88), or « La suite » has nothing to say at B2.
- **Closing it again is a silent reset** (#74): `readView` filters on `CHOOSABLE_LEVELS`, so B2
  drops out of every view that held it. **Do not open it to see what it looks like.**
- **The chooser rates nothing** (#77): a B2 with nothing of its own looks empty, and nothing
  says so. If the site is ever listed, the « en cours » note comes back first.
