# B1

Writing at B1: what B1 is here, the page's language, its tags, and what it is measured against.
**`src/data/navigation.ts` is the truth about what exists.** The other rungs: [`a1.md`](a1.md),
[`a2.md`](a2.md), [`b2.md`](b2.md).

**B1 is offered while being written** (#74). A learner who wants the A2 pages too keeps A2 in view
(#86).

## What B1 material is

Two kinds, and nothing else:

- **A new point whose floor is B1** — a page with `level: "B1"`, in the B1 parcours after what it
  builds on (#88). The first ones are in `grammaire`; count them in the
  manifest.
- **A harder set on a page with no floor** — the B1 bank of a drill, the B1 questions of a
  `lecture` text (`sets`, #68, #87). How: `lesson-author.md` (« The harder set asks more of the
  same page »). **Read the A2 set before writing the B1 one, every time.**
- **The B1 body of a literary work** (#92) — `b1.tsx` beside `a2.tsx` on the same page: the
  passage without cuts, or its companion text, with its own questions.

**Never a B1 copy of an A2 page** (#86): the A2 page is one tick of the view away.

**Not every text can carry a B1 set honestly** — past a point a harder question is trivia. List the
sets a page can actually serve.

## The page is French only (#53)

Everything in [`a2.md`](a2.md) holds. **The explanation may use what A2 taught, never the point
on the page**: a page on *ce dont* may not explain itself with *dont*.

## How a page is tagged

- **`level: "B1"`** — listed to whoever keeps B1 in view (#86).
- **A page with `sets` lists exactly the levels it has material for** — `["A2", "B1"]`, or
  `["A1", "A2", "B1"]` on a literary work (#92).
  Adding a B1 set to a page that has none is a key change (AGENTS.md §8).
- **A DELF épreuve is `level: "B1"`**, and ends the B1 parcours as its `exam` (#89).
- **A `delf` descriptor is per level** on a page with `sets` — `{ A2: '…', B1: '…' }` — because it
  says what that level's questions check.

## What it is measured against

**DELF B1** (#15), from the same *Inventaire* Annexe E. **B1 has no syllabus map** (AGENTS.md
§12.2). **Before a batch of B1 work, write it here** — FONCTIONS as the gate, the GRAMMAIRE points,
and the page covering each — so the order comes from the syllabus.
