# A2

Writing at A2: who reads it, the page's language, its tags, and what it is measured against.
**`src/data/navigation.ts` is the truth about what exists.** The other rungs: [`a1.md`](a1.md),
[`b1.md`](b1.md), [`b2.md`](b2.md).

**A2 is written first** (#74), so the briefs in `.claude/agents/` already describe an A2 page; this
file holds only what is specific to the rung.

## Who reads it

**The learner** who has the basics and is building the past and the pronouns. **The heritage
speaker is not an A2** (#13): their pages (`orthographe`, `astuces`, `conjugaison`) answer to
literacy, mostly tagged `ANY`.

## The page is French only (#53)

- **The explanation is easier than the French it teaches** — the first thing
  `content-proofreader` hunts. Présent, passé composé, imparfait, futur proche; no subjunctive, no
  literary tenses, no metalanguage beyond *verbe, sujet, adjectif, accord*.
- **The `.rule` box is where it fails**: no `dont`, no `lequel`, nothing three clauses long.
- **False friends defined, wrong-then-right in French, no Spanish anywhere** — AGENTS.md §1. Spanish
  is A1's (#85) and `traduction`'s source text (#55), and nothing else's.

## How a page is tagged

- **`from("A2")`** — the normal value: listed at A2, B1 and B2 (#76).
- **A `perLevel` page writes its levels out** — `A2B1`, never `from()` (#76); adding or removing
  `perLevel` is a data migration (AGENTS.md §8).
- **A DELF épreuve is `["A2"]`**: a B1 candidate sits the B1 paper.
- **The six points whose floor is A2 have no A1 twin to write** (#72); everything else shared with
  A1 owes one — both lists are in [`a1.md`](a1.md).
- **In a chapter, A2 pages sit after A1's and before B1's** — the chapter's order is the course's
  (#72).

## What it is measured against

**DELF A2 is what "done" means** (#15), from the same *Inventaire* Annexe E as A1. **A2 is not
mapped against the manifest the way A1 is.** When "what does A2 still need?" comes up, write that
map here — FONCTIONS as the gate, as [`a1.md`](a1.md) does — rather than answering from memory.
