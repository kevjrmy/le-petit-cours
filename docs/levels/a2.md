# A2

The context for writing at A2: who reads it, the page's language, how it is tagged, and what it is
measured against. **`src/data/navigation.ts` is the truth about what exists** — count there, not
here. The other rungs: [`a1.md`](a1.md), [`b1.md`](b1.md), [`b2.md`](b2.md).

**A2 is the rung the course was started at, and it is written first** (#74). Most of the course is
here, so most of the house style — the briefs in `.claude/agents/` — describes an A2 page already.
This file holds only what is specific to the rung.

## Who reads it

**The learner** who has the basics and is building the past and the pronouns. **The heritage
speaker is not an A2** (#13): plausibly oral C1 and written A2 at once, and their pages
(`orthographe`, `astuces`, `conjugaison`) answer to literacy, mostly tagged `ANY` rather than a rung.

## The page is French only (#53)

- **The explanation is easier than the French it teaches** — the one failure mode of writing in
  French, and the first thing `content-proofreader` hunts. Présent, passé composé, imparfait, futur
  proche; no subjunctive, no literary tenses, no metalanguage beyond *verbe, sujet, adjectif,
  accord*.
- **The `.rule` box is where it fails**: no `dont`, no `lequel`, nothing three clauses long.
- **A false friend gets a French definition and an example**, never a translation.
- **Wrong-then-right, in French**: « il est trois » is printed and corrected; *son las tres* is not.
- **No Spanish anywhere** — no gloss, no column, no *(es: …)*. Spanish is A1's (#85) and
  `traduction`'s source text (#55), and nothing else's.

## How a page is tagged

- **`from("A2")`** — the normal value: listed at A2, B1 and B2 (#76). Widening it never moves a tick.
- **A page with one body of work per level is `perLevel: true` with its levels written out** —
  `A2B1` in the manifest, never `from()` (#76). Adding or removing `perLevel` is a data migration
  (AGENTS.md §8); widening `levels` is not.
- **A DELF épreuve is `["A2"]`**: a B1 candidate sits the B1 paper.
- **The six points whose floor is A2** — l'imparfait, l'alternance avec le passé composé, COD/COI, la
  comparaison, EN et Y, les relatifs — **have no A1 twin to write** (#72). Everything else shared
  with A1 (négation, passé composé, futur proche, démonstratifs, partitif) owes one; see
  [`a1.md`](a1.md).
- **In a chapter, A2 pages sit after A1's and before B1's** — the chapter's order is the course's
  (#72).

## What it is measured against

**DELF A2 is what "done" means** (#15). The source is the same as A1's: Annexe E of the *Inventaire
linguistique des contenus clés des niveaux du CECRL* (CIEP / Eaquals, 2015), one page per level.
**A2 is not mapped against the manifest the way A1 is.** When the question "what does A2 still
need?" comes up, write that map here — FONCTIONS as the gate, as A1 does — rather than answering it
from memory of the chapters.
